Why Industry Expertise Matters

Generic Cybersecurity Advice Is Not Enough

Every industry faces a distinct combination of regulatory mandates, threat actors, data types, and operational constraints. A banking CISO and a healthcare CIO have fundamentally different problems — and they need advisors who understand the difference.

A bank faces SAMA, CBUAE, MAS, SBP, and QCB regulations, payment fraud threats, and the need to protect transaction data at scale. A healthcare organization must protect patient records under frameworks like ISO 27799, faces ransomware as a critical threat, and must maintain clinical system availability. A SaaS company must satisfy enterprise customer security questionnaires, achieve SOC 2 Type II, and build security into its software development lifecycle.

Cyberox consultants bring sector-specific knowledge accumulated through dozens of engagements across each vertical — which means faster assessments, more relevant recommendations, and compliance programs that actually fit how your business works.
