SaaS Security — Common Questions

Should a SaaS company do SOC 2 or ISO 27001 first?+
It depends on customer expectations. US enterprise buyers often ask for SOC 2, while international and regulated buyers often value ISO 27001. Cyberox can design one control set that supports both.
Can you help with security questionnaires before certification?+
Yes. We build a questionnaire response library and evidence pack so sales teams can answer accurately while the formal certification roadmap is underway.
Do SaaS companies need ISO 27017 or ISO 27018?+
They are useful when enterprise buyers ask detailed cloud security and privacy questions. They strengthen your ISO 27001 and SOC 2 evidence for cloud-hosted platforms.
