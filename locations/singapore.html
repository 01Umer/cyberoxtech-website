FAQs

Singapore Compliance — Common Questions

What are MAS TRM Guidelines and who must comply?+
The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) Technology Risk Management (TRM) Guidelines set out MAS's expectations for financial institutions in managing technology risks, including cybersecurity risks. They apply to all MAS-regulated entities including banks, insurers, capital market intermediaries, payment service providers, and finance companies. The TRM Guidelines cover areas including technology risk governance, IT audit, access controls, cyber resilience, software development, and technology outsourcing. While the guidelines are not legally binding regulations, MAS supervisors assess compliance during routine examinations and non-compliance can result in regulatory action.
What does Singapore's PDPA require?+
Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) governs the collection, use, disclosure, and care of personal data. Key obligations include: obtaining consent before collecting personal data (with some exceptions), informing individuals of the purpose of collection, allowing access and correction requests, implementing reasonable security measures to protect personal data, and notifying the PDPC and affected individuals in the event of a data breach that causes or is likely to cause significant harm. Organisations must appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO). PDPA amendments in 2021 significantly strengthened breach notification requirements and introduced mandatory financial penalties for serious breaches.
Is ISO 27001 required for MAS-regulated entities in Singapore?+
ISO 27001 is not explicitly mandated by MAS regulations, but it is strongly recommended and widely adopted by MAS-regulated financial institutions. ISO 27001 implementation significantly aids compliance with MAS TRM Guidelines, as the two frameworks share substantial overlap in governance, risk management, access control, incident management, and business continuity requirements. Many Singapore financial institutions pursue ISO 27001 certification to demonstrate a systematic, internationally-recognized approach to information security management to regulators, clients, and counterparties. Cyberox designs ISO 27001 programs with integrated MAS TRM alignment to deliver dual-framework compliance efficiently.
How long does MAS TRM compliance implementation take?+
MAS TRM compliance implementation timelines depend heavily on the organization's starting security maturity and the extent of control gaps identified in the initial assessment. For organizations with basic security controls already in place, significant TRM compliance progress can typically be achieved within 12–20 weeks. For organizations starting from a low baseline, 6–12 months is more realistic for comprehensive TRM compliance. Cyberox always begins with a TRM gap assessment to provide a realistic timeline and budget estimate. Where combined with ISO 27001 certification, we leverage the significant overlap between the two frameworks to minimize total effort and cost.
