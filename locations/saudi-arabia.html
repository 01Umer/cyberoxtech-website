Regulatory Landscape

Saudi Arabia's Cybersecurity Regulatory Environment

Saudi Arabia has one of the world's most ambitious national cybersecurity frameworks, driven by Vision 2030's digital transformation agenda and the NCA's comprehensive control requirements.

🛡️

NCA ECC Framework

The National Cybersecurity Authority's Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC) are mandatory for all government entities and critical national infrastructure operators. The ECC framework consists of 5 main domains and 29 cybersecurity controls covering governance, protection, defense, resilience, and third-party management.

📡

CITC Requirements

The Communications and Information Technology Commission sets cybersecurity requirements for licensed telecommunications and ICT service providers operating in the Kingdom. We assist CITC-licensed organizations with security compliance and certification.

🏦

SAMA Cybersecurity Framework

The Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) has issued a comprehensive Cyber Security Framework mandatory for all banks, insurance companies, and financial market infrastructure entities. The framework covers governance, risk management, operations, and third-party security.

⚙️

CST Regulations

The Communications, Space and Technology Commission has issued additional cybersecurity requirements relevant to technology service providers. We help organizations understand and comply with CST cybersecurity obligations across all applicable dimensions.

🚀

Vision 2030 Digital Security

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 digital transformation agenda creates significant cybersecurity obligations for organizations in smart city projects, digital government, NEOM, and related initiatives. We support the security requirements of Vision 2030 digital projects.

🏆

ISO 27001 Alignment

ISO 27001 aligns strongly with NCA ECC and SAMA requirements. Many Saudi organizations pursue ISO 27001 certification as a means of demonstrating NCA ECC compliance. We leverage this alignment to deliver efficient, dual-framework compliance programs.

FAQs

Saudi Arabia Compliance — Common Questions

Who must comply with NCA ECC in Saudi Arabia?+
NCA ECC compliance is mandatory for all Saudi government entities, government-related entities (GREs), and operators of critical national infrastructure (CNI). This includes ministries, government agencies, state-owned enterprises, telecommunications companies, energy and utilities providers, financial institutions regulated by SAMA, and healthcare organizations designated as critical infrastructure. Private sector entities that provide services to government or CNI organizations should also ensure NCA ECC alignment as it is frequently required by their public sector clients as a procurement condition.
What does the SAMA Cybersecurity Framework require?+
The Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) Cyber Security Framework is mandatory for all entities licensed by SAMA, including banks, insurance companies, finance companies, and financial market infrastructure providers. The framework is structured around four maturity levels and covers cybersecurity leadership and governance, cybersecurity risk management and compliance, cybersecurity operations and technology, and third-party cybersecurity. Organizations are required to self-assess against the framework annually and submit results to SAMA. Cyberox conducts SAMA CSF gap assessments, develops remediation roadmaps, and supports annual self-assessments.
How long does NCA ECC compliance implementation take?+
NCA ECC compliance implementation timelines vary based on the organization's size, current security maturity, and the number of control gaps identified. For organizations with an existing ISO 27001 or similar framework in place, NCA ECC compliance can typically be achieved within 8–16 weeks by mapping existing controls and addressing gaps. Organizations starting from a low maturity baseline typically require 16–32 weeks. Cyberox begins all NCA ECC engagements with a rapid gap assessment to provide an accurate timeline and budget estimate.
