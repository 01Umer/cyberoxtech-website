Regulatory Landscape

Pakistan's Cybersecurity Regulatory Framework

Navigating Pakistan's evolving regulatory requirements requires specialist local knowledge. We keep our clients ahead of compliance obligations across all relevant frameworks.

📡

PTA Compliance

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority mandates security requirements for licensed operators. We assist telecoms and ISPs with PTA security directives, lawful intercept requirements, and network security frameworks.

🔐

Pakistan PDPB

Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill establishes data protection obligations for organizations handling personal data. We provide gap assessments, data mapping, privacy notices, and PDPB-readiness programs.

🏦

SECP Cybersecurity Requirements

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan has issued cybersecurity guidelines for licensed financial entities. We help NBFIs, insurers, and capital market participants meet SECP security requirements.

🏛️

SBP Cybersecurity Framework

The State Bank of Pakistan's cybersecurity framework sets mandatory requirements for banks and payment service providers. We conduct SBP-aligned security assessments and implement required controls.

FAQs

Pakistan Cybersecurity — Common Questions

Is ISO 27001 required in Pakistan?+
ISO 27001 is not universally mandated by Pakistani law, but it is effectively required in practice for many sectors. Government agencies, banks supervised by SBP, SECP-regulated entities, and large enterprise clients routinely require ISO 27001 certification from their technology and service vendors as a procurement condition. For organizations serving GCC clients or exporting IT services, ISO 27001 is frequently a contract prerequisite.
How much does ISO 27001 cost in Pakistan?+
Total ISO 27001 project cost in Pakistan typically includes consulting fees (for gap assessment, ISMS implementation, and internal audit support), certification body fees (Stage 1 and Stage 2 audit), and CyberShield platform access. Cyberox offers competitive, fixed-scope pricing tailored to the Pakistani market — significantly more affordable than international consulting firms while maintaining the same methodology and quality. Contact us for a project-specific proposal based on your organization size and current security maturity.
Do you provide on-site services across Pakistan?+
Yes. While many of our consulting activities can be delivered remotely, we regularly conduct on-site engagements in Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, and Rawalpindi. On-site workshops, gap assessment interviews, internal audits, and security awareness training sessions are available across all major Pakistani cities. We also serve clients in Peshawar, Multan, and Faisalabad on request.
What is Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill (PDPB)?+
Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill (PDPB) is legislation modeled broadly on international data protection frameworks that establishes obligations for organizations collecting, processing, and storing personal data of Pakistani residents. Key requirements include data subject rights, consent mechanisms, data breach notification, data localization provisions, and appointment of Data Protection Officers for certain entities. Cyberox provides PDPB gap assessments, data mapping exercises, and readiness programs to help organizations prepare for compliance obligations under the Bill.
