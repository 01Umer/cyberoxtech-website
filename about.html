Our Story

Born from the Need for Real Cybersecurity Expertise in Our Region

Cyberox Technologies was founded with a clear purpose: to bring world-class cybersecurity consulting and compliance expertise to Pakistan and the wider GCC region — without the Big 4 price tag or the generic IT company approach.

We saw organizations struggling with compliance requirements from frameworks like ISO 27001, SOC 2, and regional regulations — and being underserved by firms that either lacked deep security expertise or didn't understand the local regulatory context. We built Cyberox to close that gap.

Today, we serve clients across Pakistan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Singapore — from financial institutions and telecoms to government agencies and fast-growing SaaS companies. And with the CyberShield GRC Platform, we've extended our expertise into technology, giving organizations a powerful tool to manage compliance continuously.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted cybersecurity and compliance partner for organizations across Pakistan and the GCC — empowering every business to operate securely, confidently, and in compliance.

Our Mission

To deliver expert cybersecurity consulting, compliance services, and GRC technology that protects our clients' data, reduces their risk, and enables sustainable growth.
