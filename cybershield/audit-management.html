The Problem

The Audit Preparation Problem Every Organisation Faces

For most organisations, ISO 27001 or SOC 2 audit preparation is a months-long scramble. Evidence is stored across email threads, personal drives, and shared folders with no consistent structure. Controls that should be documented are missing records. Non-conformities from the last audit were "resolved" but never properly verified and closed.

The result: teams work nights and weekends before every audit cycle, auditors identify the same gaps they found last time, and the certification process costs far more in internal hours than it needs to.

CyberShield Audit Management solves this by making audit-readiness a continuous state — not a panic before every audit date.

Audit Management Features

Every Tool for Seamless Audit Preparation and Execution

📅

Audit Planning

Create structured audit plans with defined scope, objectives, criteria, and schedule. Assign audit activities to specific team members with clear deadlines. CyberShield tracks all activities in a single audit timeline view.

👥

Task Assignment

Break audit preparation into assignable tasks across your team. Each task has an owner, due date, and status. The compliance manager has a real-time view of all outstanding items without sending a single status email.

📥

Evidence Requests

Create formal evidence requests linked directly to specific controls. Team members receive clear notifications of what is needed and by when. Evidence submissions are automatically organised and linked to the right control.

🔍

Finding Tracking

Record and track every audit finding — from minor observations to major non-conformities. Assign severity ratings, owners, and resolution deadlines. Never lose track of an open finding between audit cycles.

⚠️

Non-Conformity Management

Manage the full non-conformity lifecycle: identification, root cause analysis, corrective action planning, implementation, and effectiveness review. Full audit trail maintained throughout the process.

✅

Corrective Action Plans

Create structured corrective action plans (CAPs) for every non-conformity. Track implementation milestones, assign responsible parties, and verify effectiveness before officially closing the finding.

📚

Audit History

Maintain a complete history of all past audit cycles — findings, non-conformities, corrective actions, and evidence. Demonstrate continuous improvement to certification auditors with a full historical record.

📤

Auditor-Ready Export

Generate a complete audit evidence package in one click — structured by control, with all attached evidence files, task completion records, and finding histories ready to present to your external auditor.

Framework Support

Built for ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Audits

🛡️

ISO 27001 Surveillance & Recertification

CyberShield Audit Management is structured around ISO 27001 audit requirements — from internal audit planning per Clause 9.2 through management review per Clause 9.3 and certification body audit preparation. Audit history demonstrates the required cycle of continual improvement.

✅

SOC 2 Type I & Type II

For SOC 2 audits, CyberShield helps you structure evidence collection around the Trust Service Criteria, track control operating effectiveness over the observation period, and manage auditor information requests systematically.

🔗

Connected to ISMS Hub & Evidence

Audit Management connects directly to the ISMS Hub and Evidence Management modules. Control status, evidence files, and policy versions are all accessible directly within the audit workflow — no switching between systems.
