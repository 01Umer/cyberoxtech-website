What is CyberShield?

Compliance Management Built for Real Security Teams

Most compliance teams are drowning in spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and audit-prep panic. CyberShield replaces that chaos with a single platform purpose-built for ISO 27001 and multi-framework compliance.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

CyberShield is available as a SaaS subscription priced per organisation, not per user — making it affordable for small compliance teams. We offer tiered plans based on the number of modules and frameworks you need. Contact us for a personalised quote after your demo.

Most organisations are up and running within 3–5 business days for core modules. Because CyberShield is pre-configured for ISO 27001 out of the box, you don't need a lengthy setup project. Our onboarding team provides a guided configuration session included with every subscription.

CyberShield natively supports ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 (AICPA Trust Service Criteria), NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and NCA ECC (Saudi Arabia). The Unified Control Framework module allows you to cross-map between frameworks so a single control satisfies multiple requirements simultaneously.

CyberShield is offered as a cloud SaaS platform hosted on secure, SOC 2-compliant infrastructure. For regulated industries or organisations with strict data residency requirements, we offer private cloud and on-premises deployment options. Discuss your requirements during the demo.

All CyberShield subscriptions include dedicated onboarding, email and chat support, and access to our knowledge base. For clients also working with Cyberox on ISO 27001 consulting or vCISO services, the platform is fully integrated into their advisory engagement — meaning your consultant actively uses CyberShield to manage your programme alongside you.
