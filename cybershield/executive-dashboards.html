The Challenge

The Board Reporting Problem Every CISO Faces

Boards and executive leadership need to oversee the organisation's security posture — but they do not have the time or technical background to consume detailed compliance reports. Most CISOs face the same dilemma: either produce a simplified summary that lacks the depth auditors need, or present technical reports that board members do not engage with.

Meanwhile, the data to answer every leadership question — certification status, risk exposure, training completion, upcoming audit dates, open non-conformities — exists somewhere in the organisation. The problem is getting it into one coherent view without manual effort every reporting cycle.

CyberShield Executive Dashboards pull data from every module in the platform and present it as a clean, executive-ready view that updates automatically.

What Executives See

Every Security KPI Your Leadership Team Needs

🛡️

Compliance Status

Real-time ISO 27001 and SOC 2 compliance percentages. Control implementation progress across all Annex A themes. Clear indication of certification status — certified, in progress, or overdue for renewal.

⚠️

Risk Posture

Current risk landscape at a glance: total identified risks, risk treatment status, open high-severity risks requiring executive attention, and trend direction (improving or deteriorating).

🎓

Training Completion

Organisation-wide security awareness training completion rate by department. Phishing simulation results and trend. Human risk score distribution across the organisation.

📅

Upcoming Audits & Milestones

Next internal audit date, external certification audit schedule, policy review deadlines, and management review meetings — all in a single compliance calendar view with days-to-deadline indicators.

📊

Open Non-Conformities

Count and status of open non-conformities from the last audit cycle, corrective action plan progress, and any overdue remediation items requiring executive escalation.

📈

Security Programme Trend

Month-over-month compliance score trends, risk score trajectory, and training improvement metrics — showing the board that the security programme is maturing over time.
