Centralised Repository

Control-to-Evidence Mapping

Every evidence item is mapped directly to the specific controls it satisfies. For any given Annex A control, you can see in one click exactly what evidence is attached, when it was last updated, and whether it is still valid.

Framework Evidence Requirements

What ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Require — and How CyberShield Delivers It

ISO 27001 Evidence Requirements

ISO 27001 is heavily documentation-focused. The standard requires documented evidence for control implementation, management reviews, risk assessments, internal audits, training records, and continual improvement. CyberShield's evidence repository is pre-structured around these exact requirements.

  • Documented information per Clause 7.5 — policies, procedures, records
  • Risk assessment and treatment records per Clause 6.1
  • Internal audit records per Clause 9.2
  • Management review records per Clause 9.3
  • Training and awareness records per Clause 7.2 and 7.3
  • Control implementation evidence for all 93 Annex A controls

SOC 2 Evidence Requirements

For SOC 2 Type II, evidence must demonstrate that controls operated effectively over the observation period — not just that they exist. CyberShield's version-controlled, time-stamped evidence repository supports the operating effectiveness requirement throughout the audit window.

  • Access review logs and provisioning evidence for CC6.1–CC6.3
  • Incident and change management records for CC7–CC8
  • Risk management documentation for CC3
  • Vendor management evidence for CC9
  • Availability and monitoring evidence for A1 criteria
  • Timestamped history demonstrating continuous control operation
