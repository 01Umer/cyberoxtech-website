The Problem

Managing Multiple Frameworks Is Causing Unnecessary Duplication

Most growing organisations face pressure from multiple directions: ISO 27001 for enterprise contracts, SOC 2 for US customers, NIST CSF for government work, and NCA ECC for Saudi regulatory compliance. Each framework has its own control set — and without a unified approach, organisations end up managing them in parallel, duplicating effort on controls that substantially overlap.

Studies show that ISO 27001 and SOC 2 share over 80% of their underlying control requirements. Yet without a unified control framework, most organisations manage them as completely separate compliance programmes — doubling their workload.

CyberShield's Unified Control Framework eliminates this duplication by identifying the overlaps and allowing a single control implementation to satisfy requirements across multiple frameworks simultaneously.

How It Works

Cross-Framework Control Mapping — Automatic and Pre-Built

CyberShield's UCF comes pre-loaded with cross-mapping tables built by our certified compliance experts. You do not need to manually research which ISO 27001 controls correspond to which SOC 2 Trust Service Criteria — it is already done.

1️⃣

Implement Once

Implement a control in the ISMS Hub — such as access control policy or encryption standard — and mark it as complete with supporting evidence attached.

2️⃣

Automatic Cross-Mapping

CyberShield's UCF automatically identifies which requirements in ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST CSF, and NCA ECC are satisfied by that single control implementation — with the mapping clearly displayed.

3️⃣

See Your Multi-Framework Coverage

The UCF dashboard shows your compliance status across all active frameworks simultaneously — so you can see your ISO 27001 coverage and SOC 2 readiness in the same view, sharing the same evidence.
