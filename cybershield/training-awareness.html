The Problem

Why Most Security Awareness Training Fails

Annual security training videos watched by half-engaged employees is not a security programme. It is a compliance tick-box. Research consistently shows that generic, one-size-fits-all training has minimal impact on security behaviour — and yet most organisations still rely on it.

The problems are predictable: content that is not relevant to the employee's role, no reinforcement between annual cycles, no measurement of whether behaviour actually changed, and nothing connecting training to real threat simulations.

CyberShield was built to solve all of these problems in one integrated platform.

Training That Actually Changes Security Behaviour

Role-Based Modules

Phishing Simulations

Mobile-Friendly

✅

Completion Tracking

Admins see completion status across every employee, team, and department in real time. Automated reminders go to employees who have not completed assigned modules. No manual chasing needed.
