What the ISMS Hub Does

Stop Managing Your ISMS in Spreadsheets

Most organisations manage their Information Security Management System across a tangle of spreadsheets, Word documents, and shared drives. Clauses are in one file, controls in another, policies in a folder nobody updates, and the risk register in a spreadsheet from three years ago.

The ISMS Hub centralises everything. It gives your compliance team one authoritative place to manage the entire ISO 27001 lifecycle — from initial control implementation through SoA sign-off, internal audits, and certification renewal.

Key Features

Everything Your ISO 27001 Programme Needs

🎛️

Control Tracking

Track the implementation status of all 93 Annex A controls. Assign owners, set due dates, link evidence, and monitor progress across your entire control set in real time.

📄

Policy Repository

Store, version, and manage all your information security policies in one place. Set review schedules, track approvals, and maintain a full audit trail of every policy change.

⚠️

Risk Register Integration

Maintain a live risk register that links directly to the controls designed to treat each risk. See at a glance which risks are adequately controlled and which need attention.

🗺️

Clause Mapping

Map every control, policy, and procedure directly to the ISO 27001:2022 clauses they satisfy. Always know your clause coverage status — ready for any auditor question.

📝

Statement of Applicability (SoA)

Generate and maintain your Statement of Applicability automatically. Include justifications for each included and excluded control, and export a formatted SoA for your certification audit.

📅

ISMS Calendar & Milestones

Track management review meetings, internal audit schedules, policy review dates, and certification renewal deadlines — all in one compliance calendar with automated reminders.
