What is CyberShield X?

Enterprise Compliance — Without Enterprise Complexity

CyberShield X is the full-capability tier of the CyberShield platform, designed for organizations running active compliance programs across multiple frameworks simultaneously.

Where standard GRC tools require you to configure frameworks from scratch, CyberShield X ships pre-mapped for ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 TSC, NIST CSF 2.0, and NCA ECC. Controls are unified — one control can satisfy requirements across all four frameworks at once, eliminating duplicated effort.

CyberShield X adds AI-assisted control gap detection, advanced human risk scoring across your entire workforce, automated evidence collection, and board-ready executive reporting on top of the core platform modules.

Key Capabilities

Built for Teams That Manage Compliance Seriously

CyberShield X is designed for compliance managers, CISOs, and security teams who need a reliable, auditable system of record — not another spreadsheet.

🗺️

Multi-Framework Control Mapping

Map a single control to ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST CSF, and NCA ECC simultaneously. Satisfy four frameworks with one evidence set — eliminating duplicated assessments and audit preparation work.

🧠

AI-Assisted Gap Detection

CyberShield X analyzes your current control implementations against framework requirements and surfaces gaps before your auditor does. Prioritized by risk impact, not alphabetical order.

👥

Human Risk Scoring

Every employee gets a live risk score based on phishing susceptibility, training completion, policy acknowledgment, and behavioral signals. Identify your highest-risk individuals before they become incidents.

📂

Automated Evidence Collection

Connect CyberShield X to your infrastructure and evidence is collected automatically — no more manual screenshots and file uploads before every audit. Auditors get a live evidence trail, not a ZIP file.

📊

Executive Reporting

Board-ready dashboards that translate your compliance posture into business language. Risk exposure, control coverage, open findings, and audit status — in one page your CEO can read in two minutes.

🚨

Audit-Ready at All Times

CyberShield X maintains a continuous, tamper-evident audit trail. When your certification body or internal auditors arrive, your evidence is organized, timestamped, and ready — no scramble.
