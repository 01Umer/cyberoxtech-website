What is a vCISO?

A CISO You Can Afford — Without Hiring One Full-Time

A Virtual CISO (vCISO) is an experienced security executive who provides strategic leadership, oversight, and program management for your cybersecurity function — on a part-time or fractional basis.

A full-time CISO in Pakistan or the UAE commands significant salary, benefits, and overhead. For most small and mid-size organizations, that investment is difficult to justify. A vCISO gives you the same expertise and strategic value at a fraction of the cost, with the flexibility to scale engagement up or down as your needs change.

Your Cyberox vCISO integrates with your leadership team — attending board meetings, owning your security roadmap, and ensuring your security program matures alongside your business.

FAQs

vCISO — Common Questions

How is a vCISO different from a full-time CISO?+
A full-time CISO is an employee dedicated entirely to your organization. A vCISO provides the same strategic leadership and expertise on a part-time or fractional basis, typically serving multiple clients. The key difference is cost and flexibility — a vCISO costs a fraction of a full-time hire and can be scaled up or down as your needs change. For most SMEs and growing companies, a vCISO delivers better value because you get senior expertise without the overhead of a full executive salary and benefits package.
How much does a vCISO cost compared to hiring a full-time CISO?+
A full-time CISO in Pakistan or the UAE typically costs PKR 800K–2M+ per month (or AED 25,000–60,000) in salary alone, plus benefits, bonuses, and overhead. A Cyberox vCISO retainer typically costs 20–40% of that, depending on the engagement tier and scope. Contact us for specific pricing based on your organization's size and requirements.
What are the main responsibilities of a vCISO?+
A vCISO is responsible for your overall security strategy and program — not just day-to-day technical operations. This includes: developing and maintaining the security roadmap, reporting to the board and executive team, owning the risk management process, managing compliance programs (ISO 27001, SOC 2, etc.), leading incident response when events occur, overseeing policy development, and managing vendor security relationships. The vCISO is your accountability point for security — ensuring the right things get done and that leadership is informed.
Can a vCISO satisfy regulatory requirements for a named CISO?+
In most cases, yes. For regulatory requirements that specify a named information security officer or CISO, our vCISO can serve in that role, with a contractual agreement that defines their responsibilities and accountability. We recommend confirming specific regulatory language with us during the discovery call, as requirements vary by jurisdiction and sector (e.g., SECP in Pakistan, CBUAE in UAE have different expectations).
What happens if we eventually want to hire a full-time CISO?+
Our vCISO service is designed with eventual transition in mind. When you're ready to hire internally, we assist with the recruitment brief, interview process, and onboarding of the new hire. We document all security programs, processes, and institutional knowledge so your incoming CISO inherits a well-organized, mature program — not a blank slate.
