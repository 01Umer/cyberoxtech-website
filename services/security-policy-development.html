Why Policies Matter

The Documented Foundation of Your Security Program

ISO 27001 and SOC 2 both require documented information security policies. But their value goes far beyond compliance — policies define expected behaviour, establish accountability, and provide the reference point for every security decision.

Without clear, current, approved policies, security expectations are ambiguous. Staff make security decisions based on assumptions. Auditors find gaps immediately. And when incidents occur, the absence of a clear policy becomes a liability.

Cyberox develops policies that are readable, organization-specific, and actually used — not downloaded templates that gather digital dust.

ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Policy Requirements:

ISO 27001 Clause 5.2 requires a documented information security policy. SOC 2 Common Criteria require evidence of security policies covering the relevant Trust Service Criteria. Both frameworks require policies to be approved by management, communicated to staff, and reviewed at regular intervals.

FAQs

Policy Development — Common Questions

Can't we just use template policies from the internet?+
Generic templates are a starting point, but they consistently fail auditors. ISO 27001 auditors look for evidence that policies reflect your actual organization — your specific systems, roles, processes, and context. Generic templates typically contain placeholder text, irrelevant sections, and language that doesn't match how your organization actually operates. More importantly, templates aren't owned — no one feels responsible for enforcing them. Cyberox policies are tailored, reviewed with your team, and built to be lived by rather than filed away.
How long does it take to develop a complete policy library?+
A complete 20+ policy library typically takes 3–5 weeks from kick-off to management approval, including our development process, stakeholder review cycles, and approval documentation. We work in batches — typically delivering foundation and high-priority policies first so you can meet early certification milestones. Individual policies with a defined scope can often be delivered in under a week.
How many policies do we actually need for ISO 27001?+
ISO 27001 mandates specific documented information at Clause 5.2 (the Information Security Policy), plus documented information for risk assessment, risk treatment, the SoA, and management review. Beyond these mandated documents, auditors expect to see supporting policies for the Annex A controls you've included in your SoA. Most organizations need 15–25 policies to support a comprehensive ISO 27001 ISMS. The exact number depends on your scope and which controls are applicable.
What is the difference between a policy and a procedure?+
A policy states what must be done and why — it's a governance-level document approved by management. A procedure describes how it is done — step-by-step instructions for a specific activity. For ISO 27001, you need both: the policy establishes the requirement, and the procedure tells staff how to fulfill it. Cyberox develops both policies and supporting procedures as part of our standard engagement.
