Why ISO 27701

Turn Privacy Obligations Into a Governed Operating Model

Privacy laws require more than consent banners and policy pages. They require governance, accountability, data inventory, risk management, processor oversight, breach response, and evidence that controls are actually operating.

ISO 27701 gives your organization a structured Privacy Information Management System (PIMS) that builds on ISO 27001. It is especially valuable for SaaS companies, fintechs, healthcare providers, banks, outsourcing providers, and any organization handling personal data across borders.

Cyberox maps your existing ISMS controls to ISO 27701 requirements, identifies privacy-specific gaps, and builds a practical implementation roadmap that reduces duplication across GDPR, UAE PDPL, PDPA, and customer due diligence requests.

FAQs

ISO 27701 — Common Questions

Do we need ISO 27001 before ISO 27701?+
ISO 27701 is an extension to ISO 27001, so it works best when an ISMS is already implemented or being implemented in parallel. Cyberox can combine both projects into one integrated roadmap.
Does ISO 27701 replace GDPR or UAE PDPL compliance?+
No. ISO 27701 is not a law, but it gives a recognized management system structure for meeting many privacy governance and accountability requirements found in GDPR, UAE PDPL, Singapore PDPA, and similar privacy regulations.
Can processors use ISO 27701?+
Yes. ISO 27701 includes requirements for both controllers and processors. We scope your role in each processing activity so the right controls and evidence are implemented.
