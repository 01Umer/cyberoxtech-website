Frameworks We Cover

Regional Regulatory Frameworks We Know Inside Out

Cyberox consultants have deep expertise in the regulatory landscape across Pakistan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Singapore.

🇸🇦

NCA ECC — Saudi Arabia

The National Cybersecurity Authority's Essential Cybersecurity Controls are mandatory for all government entities and critical infrastructure operators in Saudi Arabia. We conduct NCA ECC gap assessments, design compliance programs, and prepare organizations for NCA assessments.

🇦🇪

UAE IA Regulation

The UAE Information Assurance Regulation (NESA) applies to critical infrastructure operators in the UAE. We map controls against UAE IA requirements and integrate compliance with ISO 27001 programs for maximum efficiency.

🔒

UAE PDPL

The UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) establishes comprehensive data protection requirements for organizations processing personal data in the UAE. We assess data flows, implement privacy controls, and develop PDPL-compliant data governance frameworks.

🇸🇬

PDPA — Singapore

Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act applies to organizations that collect, use, or disclose personal data. We support PDPA compliance assessments, DPO (Data Protection Officer) advisory, and PDPA-aligned privacy programs — often combined with ISO 27701 implementation.

🇵🇰

Pakistan PDPB

Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill establishes data protection requirements for organizations operating in Pakistan. We help organizations assess readiness and implement controls ahead of the regulation's full enforcement.

🏦

Sector Regulations

We support banking and financial services compliance with sector-specific cybersecurity requirements from SECP (Pakistan), SBP (State Bank of Pakistan), CBUAE (UAE Central Bank), and other regulators across the financial, healthcare, and telecoms sectors.

FAQs

Compliance Services — Common Questions

Does achieving ISO 27001 help with NCA ECC compliance?+
Yes — significantly. NCA ECC shares substantial overlap with ISO 27001 controls. Organizations that have implemented ISO 27001 typically find they have addressed 60–70% of NCA ECC requirements through their existing ISMS. Cyberox produces a cross-mapping between your ISO 27001 controls and NCA ECC requirements, identifying the remaining gaps efficiently. Many Saudi organizations pursue ISO 27001 and NCA ECC alignment in parallel for maximum efficiency.
Is UAE PDPL similar to GDPR?+
Yes — UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) draws significant inspiration from GDPR, including similar principles around lawfulness of processing, data subject rights, breach notification, and accountability. Key differences include the enforcement mechanism, sector exemptions, and cross-border transfer rules. Organizations already GDPR-compliant typically find UAE PDPL compliance more straightforward — but local nuances require attention.
When will Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill become law?+
Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill has been in progress for several years and is expected to be enacted. The timeline has shifted, but the direction of travel is clear — Pakistan will have a formal data protection law. Organizations that prepare now will face a smoother transition when the bill passes. Cyberox monitors regulatory developments and provides timely guidance to clients operating in Pakistan.
Can you help us comply with multiple frameworks at the same time?+
Yes — this is one of Cyberox's key strengths. We map requirements across frameworks to identify overlapping controls, eliminating the need to build separate compliance programs for each regulation. A single, well-designed ISMS can simultaneously address ISO 27001, NCA ECC, UAE IA Regulation, and data protection requirements. Our cross-framework approach significantly reduces total compliance cost and organizational burden.
