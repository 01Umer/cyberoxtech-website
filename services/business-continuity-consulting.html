ISO 22301

The International Standard for Business Continuity

ISO 22301 is the globally recognized standard for Business Continuity Management Systems (BCMS). It provides a framework for planning, establishing, implementing, operating, monitoring, reviewing, and improving a BCMS.

ISO 22301 aligns naturally with ISO 27001 — many organizations implement both together. ISO 27001 Annex A Controls 5.29, 5.30, and 8.13 all reference business continuity requirements, making an ISO 22301-aligned BCP a direct input to your ISO 27001 control set.

Key BCP Terms Defined

RTO — Recovery Time Objective
The maximum time allowed to restore a function or system after disruption
RPO — Recovery Point Objective
Maximum tolerable data loss measured in time (e.g., "up to 4 hours of data")
MTD — Maximum Tolerable Downtime
The maximum time a function can be unavailable before causing unacceptable business impact
BIA — Business Impact Analysis
Process of identifying critical functions, their dependencies, and the impact of their disruption
FAQs

Business Continuity — Common Questions

What is the difference between BCP and disaster recovery?+
Business Continuity Planning (BCP) is the broader discipline — covering all aspects of maintaining or recovering critical business functions after any type of disruption, including loss of staff, facilities, or suppliers. Disaster Recovery (DR) is a subset of BCP focused specifically on IT systems and data — restoring technology infrastructure after a disaster or major failure. A complete resilience program needs both.
How do tabletop exercises work?+
A tabletop exercise is a facilitated discussion where key stakeholders walk through a realistic disruption scenario — typically a ransomware attack, major system outage, or site evacuation. No actual systems are involved; it's a structured conversation about what you would do. Our facilitator introduces the scenario in stages, asking probing questions to test your plans. The output is a list of gaps, clarifications needed, and improvements to make to your BCP or DR plans.
Is ISO 22301 certification required?+
ISO 22301 certification is not universally required, but it is mandated or strongly encouraged for financial institutions in some jurisdictions (UAE, Singapore), critical infrastructure operators, and organizations that supply services to government. Many organizations pursue ISO 22301-aligned BCPs without formal certification — the structure and rigor of the standard is valuable regardless. Cyberox can help you achieve either aligned or certified status depending on your requirements.
How does BCP relate to ISO 27001?+
ISO 27001:2022 requires business continuity considerations in multiple Annex A controls: A.5.29 (Information security during disruption), A.5.30 (ICT readiness for business continuity), and A.8.13 (Information backup). Having an ISO 22301-aligned BCP in place satisfies these requirements directly and provides strong evidence during Stage 2 certification audits. Cyberox can deliver both programs together for maximum efficiency.
