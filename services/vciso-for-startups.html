FAQs

vCISO for Startups — Common Questions

At what stage does a startup need a vCISO?+
Most startups engage a vCISO when they face a concrete trigger: an enterprise deal requiring ISO 27001 or SOC 2, an investor due diligence request, or a customer security questionnaire they can't answer. Pre-seed companies typically don't need one yet. Seed-stage companies often need basic policies and questionnaire support. Series A and beyond typically need a formal program — ISO 27001, SOC 2, or both.
How quickly can we get ISO 27001 certified?+
For a startup with limited existing controls, ISO 27001 certification typically takes 4–6 months from engagement start to certificate. We've achieved first-attempt certification in as few as 14 weeks for startups with good technical foundations. The timeline depends heavily on your starting maturity and the complexity of your operations. Your vCISO will set a realistic timeline during the gap assessment in month one.
Can a startup vCISO handle customer security questionnaires?+
Yes — this is one of the highest-value things a startup vCISO does. We maintain a master questionnaire library covering CAIQ, SIG, and custom enterprise formats. When a prospect sends a questionnaire, your vCISO completes it, coordinates with your engineering team for technical questions, and reviews every answer before submission. Accurate, professional, fast — so your sales cycle doesn't stall.
What's the cost difference between a vCISO and hiring in-house?+
A full-time CISO hire typically costs PKR 1.5M–3M/month (or AED 30,000–60,000) in salary alone, plus recruitment costs, benefits, and onboarding time of 3–6 months. A Cyberox vCISO retainer for a startup typically costs 20–30% of that, starts in one week, and includes access to the full Cyberox team — not just one individual. Contact us for specific pricing based on your scope.
