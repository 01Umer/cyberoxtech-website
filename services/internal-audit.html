Why Internal Audits Matter

The Audit That Prevents a Failed Audit

Many organizations treat the internal audit as a formality — a quick self-check before the real audit. This is a costly mistake.

An ISO 27001 internal audit conducted by qualified, independent auditors is one of the most valuable investments you can make in your certification process. It surfaces real non-conformities — gaps between what your ISMS says it does and what it actually does — before your certification body finds them.

Non-conformities discovered during a Stage 2 certification audit can delay certification, increase costs, and damage credibility. Non-conformities discovered during your own internal audit are an opportunity to improve.

ISO 27001 Clause 9.2 Requirements

Clause 9.2 of ISO 27001 requires organizations to conduct internal audits at planned intervals to provide information on whether the ISMS:

  • ✓
    Conforms to the organization's own requirements for its ISMS
  • ✓
    Conforms to the requirements of ISO 27001
  • ✓
    Is effectively implemented and maintained
Auditors must be objective and impartial — meaning the internal audit must be conducted by someone independent of the areas being audited. Using Cyberox ensures true independence.
Our Process

The Cyberox Internal Audit Process

A rigorous four-phase audit process that delivers the independent, documented evidence ISO 27001 requires.

1

Audit Planning

Define audit scope, criteria, and schedule. Develop an audit plan and checklist aligned to ISO 27001:2022 clauses and Annex A controls. Communicate with auditee departments.

2

Fieldwork

Conduct interviews, document reviews, process walkthroughs, and evidence sampling. Test whether controls are implemented and operating as documented.

3

Audit Reporting

Produce a formal internal audit report documenting findings, non-conformities (major and minor), observations, and opportunities for improvement.

✓

Follow-Up

Track corrective action implementation for each non-conformity. Verify closure of findings and confirm effectiveness of corrections before the external audit.

Non-Conformity Management

Not all findings are equal. Our audit reports classify findings into:

Major Non-Conformity

Absence of, or total breakdown of, a required control. Must be closed before certification.

Minor Non-Conformity

Isolated lapse or weakness. Corrective action required with root cause analysis.

Observation

A potential risk or area for improvement, not a non-conformity. Recommended action provided.

FAQs

Internal Audit — Common Questions

Can we conduct the internal audit ourselves?+
ISO 27001 requires internal auditors to be objective and impartial — they must not audit their own work. While it is technically possible to use internal staff (trained in ISO 27001 auditing), most organizations lack the qualified resource. Using Cyberox ensures true independence, experienced audit technique, and an unbiased assessment. Certification bodies also view external internal audits more favorably as evidence of genuine objectivity.
How long does an ISO 27001 internal audit take?+
Typical duration is 2–4 audit days for a small to mid-size organization, plus 3–5 business days for report writing. The exact timeline depends on ISMS scope, number of departments covered, and the number of Annex A controls in your Statement of Applicability. We provide a detailed audit plan upfront so your team can prepare and allocate time accordingly.
When should we conduct the internal audit relative to the certification audit?+
The internal audit should be completed before the Stage 2 certification audit — typically 4–8 weeks prior — to allow sufficient time for corrective actions to be implemented and evidence of closure to be collected. The Stage 2 auditor will review your internal audit report and ask about corrective actions taken. Having a well-documented internal audit with evidence of closed non-conformities significantly strengthens your position.
Do we need an internal audit every year after certification?+
Yes. ISO 27001 Clause 9.2 requires internal audits at "planned intervals" — which, in practice, means at least annually. Annual surveillance audits by your certification body will ask to see your internal audit records and corrective action evidence. Organizations that let their internal audit program lapse risk losing certification during surveillance audits. Cyberox offers annual internal audit retainer packages for ongoing clients.
