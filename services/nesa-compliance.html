What is NESA?

Understanding the UAE National Electronic Security Authority

The National Electronic Security Authority (NESA) is the UAE federal authority responsible for cybersecurity of critical national infrastructure. Its Information Assurance Standards set mandatory security requirements for entities operating in designated critical sectors.

NESA compliance is not optional for organizations within its scope. The NESA Information Assurance Standards (IAS) define controls across 11 security domains — covering governance, risk management, asset management, physical security, access control, incident management, and business continuity — aligned with international standards including ISO 27001.

Since NESA's mandate, the UAE has also introduced the National Information Assurance (NIA) framework for federal government entities, and sector-specific guidance for financial services (CBUAE), healthcare (ADHICS), and telecommunications. Cyberox helps organizations understand and satisfy all applicable requirements.

Who Needs NESA Compliance?

Critical Sectors Subject to NESA Requirements

NESA compliance obligations apply to organizations operating within UAE's designated critical national infrastructure sectors.

🏛️

Federal Government Entities

UAE federal ministries, authorities, and government-linked entities are subject to NIA framework requirements and must implement NESA Information Assurance Standards across their information systems.

⚡

Energy & Utilities

Electricity, water, and oil & gas operators designated as critical infrastructure must demonstrate NESA compliance across operational technology (OT) and IT environments.

📡

Telecommunications

UAE telecoms operators and internet service providers face NESA requirements alongside TRA/TDRA regulatory obligations. Cyberox delivers integrated compliance covering both frameworks.

🏦

Banking & Financial Services

Financial institutions designated as critical infrastructure face NESA requirements in addition to CBUAE cybersecurity framework obligations. We align all applicable requirements in a single ISMS.

🏥

Healthcare

Hospitals and healthcare organizations managing critical patient data systems may be subject to NESA alongside ADHICS (Abu Dhabi Healthcare Information and Cyber Security Standard).

✈️

Transport & Logistics

Aviation, ports, and transport authorities operating critical national infrastructure are in scope for NESA. We deliver compliance programs tailored to OT-heavy transport environments.

FAQs

NESA Compliance — Common Questions

What is NESA compliance in UAE?+
NESA (National Electronic Security Authority) compliance refers to adherence to the UAE Information Assurance Standards issued by NESA. These standards set mandatory cybersecurity requirements for organizations operating critical national infrastructure in the UAE, including government entities, energy, telecommunications, financial services, and healthcare sectors.
Who needs to comply with NESA in UAE?+
NESA compliance is mandatory for UAE federal government entities and organizations identified as operators of critical national infrastructure. This includes entities in energy, water, telecommunications, financial services, transport, and healthcare. Private sector organizations may also be subject to NESA requirements if they operate infrastructure classified as critical by the UAE government.
What is the cost of NESA compliance in Dubai?+
NESA compliance costs vary based on your organization's size, current security maturity, the number of NESA domains in scope, and whether you are pursuing NESA alongside ISO 27001. A standalone NESA gap assessment typically takes 2–3 weeks. Full implementation projects range from 12 to 24 weeks depending on remediation scope. Cyberox provides fixed-scope, transparent pricing — contact us for a project-specific estimate.
What is the difference between NESA and NIA in UAE?+
NESA (National Electronic Security Authority) is the regulatory body, and its Information Assurance Standards (IAS) are the compliance requirements for critical infrastructure. The NIA (National Information Assurance) framework is a related set of baseline standards specifically for UAE federal government IT systems. In practice, organizations often need to address both — NESA IAS for critical infrastructure classification and NIA for government IT environments.
How does NESA relate to ISO 27001?+
NESA's Information Assurance Standards align closely with ISO 27001:2022, sharing over 70% control overlap. Organizations that implement ISO 27001 are well-positioned to satisfy most NESA requirements with incremental effort.
