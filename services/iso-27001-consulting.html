What is ISO 27001?

The Global Standard for Information Security Management

ISO 27001 is the world's leading international standard for information security management systems (ISMS). It provides a framework for organizations to systematically manage sensitive information and keep it secure.

Certification demonstrates to customers, partners, regulators, and stakeholders that your organization has implemented internationally recognized best practices to protect information. In the GCC and Pakistan, ISO 27001 is increasingly required by government agencies, banks, and enterprise clients as a condition of doing business.

Achieving ISO 27001 certification typically delivers: reduced risk of data breaches, stronger customer trust, compliance with regulatory requirements, and competitive advantage in procurement processes.

FAQs

ISO 27001 — Common Questions

How long does ISO 27001 certification take?+
For most mid-size organizations, ISO 27001 certification takes 12–24 weeks from initial gap assessment to receiving the certificate. The timeline depends on organizational size, complexity, and starting maturity level. Cyberox's structured methodology helps clients achieve certification efficiently without skipping critical steps.
What is the difference between ISO 27001 and ISO 27002?+
ISO 27001 is the certifiable management system standard — it defines the requirements your organization must meet. ISO 27002 is a supplementary code of practice that provides guidance on implementing the Annex A controls. You certify against ISO 27001; ISO 27002 helps you implement its controls effectively.
How much does ISO 27001 certification cost?+
Total cost includes consulting fees (for gap assessment, ISMS implementation, and internal audit support), certification body fees (for Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits), and any technology or tool investments. Cyberox provides transparent, fixed-scope pricing for each phase. Contact us for a project-specific proposal based on your organization's size and starting point.
Does ISO 27001 apply to all types of organizations?+
Yes. ISO 27001 is framework-agnostic and sector-agnostic — it applies to any organization that handles information, regardless of size, industry, or geography. We have implemented ISO 27001 for banks, fintechs, healthcare providers, telecoms, government agencies, SaaS companies, and professional services firms across Pakistan and the GCC.
What is the Statement of Applicability (SoA)?+
The SoA is a mandatory ISO 27001 document that lists all 93 Annex A controls, states whether each is included or excluded from your ISMS scope, and provides justification for each decision. It is one of the most scrutinized documents during a certification audit. Cyberox prepares the SoA as part of our standard engagement.
Is ISO 27001 required in Pakistan or the UAE?+
ISO 27001 is not universally mandated by law in Pakistan or UAE, but it is effectively required in practice. Government agencies, financial institutions, telecoms, and large enterprises across both countries frequently require ISO 27001 certification from their suppliers and service providers as a procurement condition. In Saudi Arabia, NCA ECC alignment often references ISO 27001 practices.
