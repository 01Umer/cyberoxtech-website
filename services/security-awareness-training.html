The Problem

Why Traditional Security Training Fails

Annual compliance tick-box training doesn't change behaviour. It produces completion certificates — not secure employees.

Traditional security awareness programs suffer from the same flaws: long, boring modules watched at double speed, content disconnected from real job roles, no measurement of whether knowledge was retained, and no follow-up on risky behaviour. Employees click "I understand" and return to clicking suspicious links.

Cyberox takes a fundamentally different approach — treating security awareness as a behaviour change program, not a compliance checkbox.

ISO 27001 Clause 7.3

Awareness is a Mandatory ISO 27001 Requirement

  • ✓
    The information security policy
  • ✓
    Their contribution to ISMS effectiveness

FAQs

Security Awareness Training — Common Questions

How is CyberShield different from other training platforms?+
CyberShield was built specifically for organizations in Pakistan and the GCC — with culturally relevant scenarios, Arabic and Urdu language support, and local compliance framework alignment (ISO 27001, NCA ECC, UAE IA Regulation). Most global platforms use generic Western phishing scenarios that don't resonate with local staff. CyberShield integrates phishing simulation, training modules, and GRC compliance tracking in a single platform, eliminating the need to manage multiple tools.
How often should phishing simulations be run?+
Best practice is monthly phishing simulations to keep staff vigilant — research shows that awareness decays quickly without regular reinforcement. CyberShield automates campaign scheduling so simulations run consistently without manual effort. Employees who click a simulated phishing link are automatically enrolled in remedial training.
What languages does CyberShield training support?+
CyberShield currently supports English, Arabic, and Urdu, with additional languages being added. Phishing simulation templates are available in multiple languages and can be customized to reflect local branding, email styles, and cultural context — making simulations more realistic and effective for regional workforces.
Can we use CyberShield as a standalone training platform without Cyberox consulting services?+
Yes — CyberShield is available as a standalone platform subscription. However, organizations pursuing ISO 27001 or other compliance frameworks benefit most from pairing CyberShield with Cyberox consulting services, as our consultants configure the platform to align with your specific compliance evidence requirements and training program design.
