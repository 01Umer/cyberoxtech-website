FAQs

ISO 27017 — Common Questions

Is ISO 27017 certifiable?+
ISO 27017 is a code of practice rather than a standalone management system. It is usually implemented alongside ISO 27001 and used to demonstrate cloud-specific control maturity to auditors and customers.
Does ISO 27017 apply to AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud?+
Yes. ISO 27017 is provider-neutral. We apply it to your actual cloud architecture and shared responsibility model regardless of cloud provider.
How does ISO 27017 help SaaS sales?+
Enterprise customers often ask detailed cloud security questions. ISO 27017 gives your team structured answers, policies, evidence, and control ownership that reduce procurement friction.
