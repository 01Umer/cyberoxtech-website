What is a Gap Assessment?

A Clear Picture of Where Your Security Stands Today

A gap assessment compares your current information security controls, policies, and practices against the requirements of a chosen framework — then shows you exactly where the gaps are.

Think of it as a security health check. You get a maturity score across every domain, a prioritized list of gaps, and a practical roadmap for remediation — without yet committing to a full certification or implementation engagement.

A gap assessment is the right first step for any organization considering ISO 27001 certification, SOC 2 readiness, regulatory compliance, or simply wanting to understand its security baseline before making investment decisions.

FAQs

Gap Assessment — Common Questions

Is the gap assessment really free?+
Yes — completely free, with no obligation to purchase any further services. We offer the gap assessment as a genuine service because we believe organizations should understand their security posture before making investment decisions. You receive a full gap report, maturity score, and remediation roadmap at no cost.
How long does the gap assessment take?+
Data collection typically takes 2–4 days and involves a structured questionnaire, document review, and interviews with key staff. You do not need to pause operations. The written report is delivered within 5 business days of completing the data collection phase. For smaller organizations, the entire process can be completed in a single week.
What information do you need from us for the assessment?+
We primarily need access to your existing policies, procedures, and system inventories (if any), plus time with your IT and security leads for structured interviews. We do not need access to your production systems, source code, or sensitive data. The assessment is documentation and interview-based, designed to be minimally disruptive.
Which framework should we assess against?+
This depends on your goals and your customers' requirements. ISO 27001 is the best starting point for most organizations pursuing certification. SOC 2 is recommended if you sell to US enterprise or tech companies. NCA ECC is required for organizations operating in Saudi Arabia's critical sectors. NIST CSF is useful for organizations wanting a comprehensive baseline without immediate certification goals. We can assess against multiple frameworks simultaneously and will guide you during the intake call.
