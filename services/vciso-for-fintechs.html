FAQs

vCISO for Fintechs — Common Questions

Does a fintech vCISO satisfy regulators like CBUAE or SAMA as a named CISO?+
In most cases, yes. CBUAE and SAMA regulations require a designated senior information security role, but do not mandate that this person be a full-time employee. A contracted vCISO with clear contractual accountability typically satisfies this requirement. We recommend confirming the specific regulatory language with us during the discovery call, as requirements vary by license type and jurisdiction.
How does a fintech vCISO handle mandatory breach notifications?+
Fintech regulators (CBUAE, SAMA, SBP) have mandatory breach notification timeframes — often 24–72 hours. Your vCISO owns the incident response process, coordinates with your technical team to assess the breach, prepares the regulator notification, and manages the follow-up investigation and remediation plan. We maintain incident response playbooks tailored to each regulatory jurisdiction.
Which fintechs need PCI DSS compliance?+
Any fintech that stores, processes, or transmits payment card data must comply with PCI DSS 4.0. This includes payment processors, e-commerce platforms, digital wallets that handle card payments, and any business that routes card transactions. The scope and level of assessment (SAQ vs. QSA audit) depends on your transaction volume and card data environment. Your vCISO will scope this during the initial gap assessment.
We operate in both UAE and Saudi Arabia. Can one vCISO cover both?+
Yes — and this is one of the areas where Cyberox specifically adds value. We have expertise in both UAE (CBUAE, VARA) and Saudi Arabia (SAMA CSF, NCA ECC) regulatory environments. Your vCISO builds a unified compliance program that satisfies both frameworks simultaneously, eliminating the duplicated effort that results from treating them as separate programs. We also have in-country presence in both markets.
