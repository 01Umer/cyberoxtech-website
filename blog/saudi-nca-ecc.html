Saudi Arabia NCA ECC Framework — What Every Organization Must Know

NCA ECC establishes essential cybersecurity controls for Saudi entities and critical suppliers.

Organizations operating in or serving Saudi Arabia should map governance, asset management, identity, operations, incident response, third-party risk, and resilience controls against ECC requirements.

Efficient approach

Use ISO 27001 as the foundation, then map ECC-specific requirements into the same evidence and risk management system.
