How to Build a Cyber Risk Register That Auditors Actually Accept

A defensible risk register connects assets, threats, vulnerabilities, impact, likelihood, treatment, owners, and residual risk.

Auditors look for a consistent method, clear criteria, approved risk appetite, and evidence that risk treatment is being tracked.

Minimum contents

Include risk statement, affected assets, business impact, existing controls, inherent score, treatment decision, treatment owner, due date, residual score, and management approval where needed.
