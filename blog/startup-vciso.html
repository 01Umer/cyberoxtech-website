When Does Your Startup Need a vCISO?

If enterprise deals, audits, investor diligence, or security incidents are stretching the founding team, fractional security leadership may be the right step.

A vCISO gives you strategy, prioritization, customer-facing security credibility, and governance without the cost of a full-time executive.

Signs you need one

You are answering complex questionnaires, preparing for SOC 2 or ISO 27001, handling regulated data, or making security decisions without a senior owner.
