SOC 2 Type I vs Type II: Which Does Your SaaS Company Actually Need?

Type I proves controls are designed at a point in time. Type II proves they operated effectively over a period, usually 3 to 12 months.

If a customer simply needs confidence that your security program exists, Type I may unblock procurement faster. If the buyer is a mature enterprise, bank, healthcare organization, or regulated company, they will usually expect Type II.

How to choose

Choose Type I when you are starting formal assurance and need a fast trust signal. Choose Type II when customers require operating effectiveness, your controls are stable, and you can maintain evidence consistently.
