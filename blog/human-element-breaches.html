Human Risk

Why Breaches Still Involve the Human Element — And How to Fix It

Security awareness works only when it changes behavior, not when it becomes an annual checkbox.

People make decisions under pressure: clicking links, approving payments, sharing files, reusing passwords, or bypassing process. A mature program measures and coaches these behaviors.

What works

Use role-based training, realistic phishing simulations, just-in-time coaching, manager reporting, and human risk scoring. CyberShield helps track training, phishing outcomes, and high-risk user patterns.
